# Statement of Work No. 1

**Between:** Ledgerline, Inc. ("Ledgerline")
**And:** Harbor Logistics Group, LLC ("Customer")
**Under:** Master Subscription Agreement dated October 1, 2026
**SOW effective date:** November 2, 2026

*This document is fictional and was written as sample data. Any resemblance to real companies is coincidental.*

## 1. Purpose

Ledgerline will implement its accounts-payable automation platform ("the Platform") for the Customer's finance team, covering invoice capture, approval workflows and payments for the Customer's three operating entities: Harbor Logistics Group (US), Harbor Freight Canada Ltd., and Harbor Warehousing LLC.

## 2. Scope of services

2.1 **Project management.** Ledgerline will assign a named implementation lead who will run a kickoff meeting within 10 business days of the SOW effective date, hold weekly status meetings, and maintain a shared project plan.

2.2 **Discovery.** Ledgerline will run up to four (4) discovery workshops of up to two hours each to document the Customer's current invoice intake, approval rules and payment processes.

2.3 **Entity and workflow configuration.** Ledgerline will configure the Platform for the three operating entities listed in Section 1, including up to fifteen (15) approval workflows in total. Approval workflows will support multi-level approval based on invoice amount, department and entity.

2.4 **ERP integration.** Ledgerline will integrate the Platform with the Customer's ERP system (Oracle NetSuite) and any other systems as reasonably required by the Customer. The NetSuite integration will sync vendors, purchase orders, GL accounts and approved bills, and will post payment status back to NetSuite.

2.5 **Purchase-order matching.** Ledgerline will configure two-way and three-way matching of invoices to purchase orders and receipts, with tolerance thresholds agreed during discovery.

2.6 **Data migration.** Ledgerline will migrate the Customer's historical vendor data into the Platform.

2.7 **Payments.** Ledgerline will enable ACH and virtual card payments for US entities. Payments for Harbor Freight Canada Ltd. will be supported in CAD via EFT.

2.8 **Single sign-on.** Ledgerline will configure SAML single sign-on with the Customer's identity provider (Okta).

2.9 **Reporting.** Ledgerline will deliver standard AP aging and approval cycle-time reports, and up to three (3) custom reports specified by the Customer.

2.10 **Training.** Ledgerline will deliver two (2) train-the-trainer sessions for up to ten (10) Customer administrators, and provide recorded end-user training videos.

2.11 **Hypercare.** Ledgerline will provide thirty (30) days of hypercare support after go-live, with a response time of four (4) business hours for priority-1 issues.

## 3. Timeline

3.1 The parties target **go-live for all three entities on January 15, 2027**.

3.2 Ledgerline's ability to meet the go-live date depends on the Customer completing its responsibilities in Section 4 on time.

## 4. Customer responsibilities

4.1 The Customer will provide a project sponsor with decision-making authority and a day-to-day project manager.

4.2 The Customer will provide NetSuite sandbox access and an administrator with integration permissions within five (5) business days of kickoff.

4.3 The Customer will deliver vendor master data, open purchase orders and approval matrices in Ledgerline's templates within ten (10) business days of the final discovery workshop.

4.4 The Customer will complete user acceptance testing (UAT) within ten (10) business days of Ledgerline making the configured Platform available for testing.

4.5 The Customer is responsible for obtaining its bank's approval to enable virtual card and ACH payments.

## 5. Acceptance

5.1 Each deliverable will be deemed accepted if the Customer does not report a material defect in writing within five (5) business days of delivery.

## 6. Out of scope

The following are out of scope unless added through a change order: integrations with systems other than NetSuite and Okta; migration of historical invoices or payment history; custom development of Platform features; on-site services; and support for entities other than those named in Section 1.

## 7. Change control

Changes to scope, timeline or fees require a written change order signed by both parties. Ledgerline will provide an estimate of effort and cost for each requested change within five (5) business days.

## 8. Fees and payment

8.1 Implementation fee: **$48,000**, fixed fee, invoiced as follows: 30% on SOW signature, 40% on completion of UAT, 30% on go-live.

8.2 Travel is not expected. Any travel requested by the Customer will be billed at cost with prior written approval.
